What We Do

DemystifyAI is a student-led, grassroots initiative that aims to make artificial intelligence more accessible to the everyday community member. Our mission is to equip Chicago community members with practical AI and digital literacy skills, empowering them to confidently participate in our AI-integrated society while protecting themselves from AI risks.
