AI Basics

Understand what Artificial Intelligence (AI) is, and isn't, in everyday terms, with examples of AI in everyday life. Explain what Large Language Models (LLMs) are and how they work, and explain Hallucinations and Bias in AI and why they happen.

  • 45–60 min
  • Instructor-Led
  • Adults & High-School Students
  • People with low-to-medium Digital Literacy

Slide 3 — What is Artificial Intelligence (AI)?

Emphasize that Artificial Intelligence (AI) is not human and uses math/statistics to work. Ask the class if they've heard of "ChatGPT" or ask them for examples of AI. Tell them that AI is a catch-all term, it represents every computer system/program that is trained to recognize patterns and make predictions. It comes in different forms, which we'll discuss in later slides.

Examples of AI: autocorrect, navigation rerouting, spam filters, social-media recommendations.

Slide 4 — AI in Your Daily Life

This visual shows how AI threads through our everyday experiences — predicting what we'll watch, buy, or type next. Each of these uses represents predictive models working quietly behind the scenes to understand what do we like, we will we do next, what do we need, etc. I often remind participants: if your phone 'knows' what you want before you do, that's not magic, it is machine learning.

Slide 5 — AI in Your Daily Life (discussion)

Optional video made by Google which provides various examples of AI in one's everyday. Ask students to brainstorm where they've used AI or watch the video and if they relate to it.

Using our materials

Our materials are open-source and yours to adapt as you see fit. However, we request that you follow these guidelines:

  1. Please inform us that you're using our curriculum, to whichever capacity, and let us know how. We're open to any feedback you have as well
  2. These materials were built following AI literacy guidelines. We also spent considerable time individually and with our partner organizations thinking through the type/substance of information everyday learners regarding AI. With that said, our materials are not complete nor perfect — please adapt them as you see fit. If you make any major structural changes however, please let us know as we'd love to improve.

By providing us feedback, you can help us improve our resources for everyone.
